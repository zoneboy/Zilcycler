Zilcycler — Account & Data Deletion

This page explains how to permanently delete your Zilcycler account and the personal data associated with it. It applies to the Zilcycler app published by Zilcycler Recyclers Ltd.

Option 1: Delete Your Account in the App (Recommended)

You can delete your account yourself, directly in the Zilcycler app:

  1. Open the Zilcycler app and log in.
  2. Go to Settings → Privacy.
  3. Tap Delete My Account.
  4. Enter your password to confirm it is really you.
  5. Enter the 6-digit verification code sent to your registered email address.

Your account is deleted immediately once the code is confirmed.

Option 2: Request Deletion by Email

If you can no longer access the app or your account, email us instead:

  • Send an email to admin@zilcycler.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".
  • Send it from the email address registered to your Zilcycler account, so we can verify the request is yours.
  • We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What Is Deleted

When your account is deleted, the following personal data is permanently deleted or anonymized:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number
  • Your home/pickup address
  • Your bank account details (used for cash redemptions)
  • Your profile information and login credentials
  • Photos you uploaded with pickup requests

What Is Kept

Some records are retained in anonymized form — meaning they can no longer be linked to you:

  • Aggregate recycling statistics (e.g. total weight of materials recycled)
  • Completed transaction records, where required for accounting, audit, or legal obligations under Nigerian law
Warning — deletion cannot be undone:
  • Any unredeemed Zoints (points) are forfeited.
  • Your recycling history and rewards cannot be recovered.
  • If you change your mind later, you will need to create a brand-new account starting from zero.
